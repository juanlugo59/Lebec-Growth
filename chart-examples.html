$5T Gap — Visualization Options

Five different ways to show the financing gap

Example 1: Stacked Proportional Visualization

A donut chart showing the proportions — philanthropy, traditional finance, and the gap — with the gap highlighted as the dominant segment.

Example 2: Animated Fill (Thermometer)

A horizontal bar that fills up with available capital, leaving the unfilled portion as a dramatic visual representation of "The Gap."

Example 3: Waffle Chart

100 squares where each represents $50B. The colored squares fill in to show what's available, leaving the rest as the gap. More visceral than bars.

Traditional Finance ($1.2T)
The Gap ($3.5T)

Large animated counters showing "Available" vs "Needed" side-by-side, with a visual deficit indicator. Simple but impactful.

Example 5: Sankey / Flow Diagram

Shows capital flowing from sources toward the $5T goal, visualizing where the money comes from and highlighting the massive shortfall.
